# Quote Writing Agreement

**Last Updated:** July 6, 2026

Welcome to QuoteDaily.

Thank you for contributing to our community. Before publishing your quote, please read this agreement carefully. By continuing to the quote editor, you acknowledge that you understand and agree to the following terms.

---

## 1. Original Content

You should only publish quotes that you created yourself or that you have the legal right to publish. Do not submit quotes copied from books, movies, songs, websites, social media, or any other copyrighted material without permission.

Submitting another person's work as your own may result in the removal of your content and restrictions on your account.

---

## 2. Community Standards

Our platform exists to inspire people through thoughtful and meaningful words.

Content containing hate speech, harassment, discrimination, threats, graphic violence, sexually explicit material, spam, scams, or illegal activities is not permitted.

We reserve the right to remove any content that violates these standards.

---

## 3. Accuracy of Information

You are responsible for the information you provide, including your display name, author information, and any optional links associated with your profile.

---

## 4. Ownership of Your Quote

You retain ownership of your original quote.

By publishing your quote on QuoteDaily, you grant us a non-exclusive license to display, distribute, promote, and archive your quote as part of our services.

This license ends if your quote is permanently removed from our platform, except where copies are required for legal, security, or archival purposes.

---

## 5. Quote Certificate

Approved quotes may receive a unique Quote ID and publication certificate containing the publication date and verification timestamp.

The certificate serves as a publication record within QuoteDaily. It is **not** an official government copyright registration and should not be interpreted as legal proof of copyright ownership.

---

## 6. Moderation

All submitted quotes may be reviewed automatically or manually before or after publication.

We may edit formatting, reject submissions, temporarily hide content, or remove published quotes if they violate our policies.

Repeated violations may lead to account suspension.

---

## 7. Changes to This Agreement

We may update these guidelines from time to time.

If significant changes are made, you may be asked to review and accept the updated agreement before publishing additional quotes.

---

## 8. Acceptance

By selecting **"I Agree"** and continuing to the quote editor, you confirm that:

- You have read this agreement.
- Your quote is your own original work.
- Your submission complies with our community guidelines.
- You understand that submissions may be reviewed before publication.
- You agree to these terms.

Thank you for helping us build a community where words inspire people every day.
